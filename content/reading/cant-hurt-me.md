## Summary
- The book starts by going over David Goggins' very difficult childhood and how we would get beat up and abused by his father who also forced him essentially into child labor. Eventually David and his mom escape but their car broke down and they had to sleep in a Hotel's conference room before reaching his grandmother's house and finally living in $8 a month subsidized housing in Brazil, Indiana.

- Goggins' struggles academically in Brazil where his reading level is a lot lower than his classmates and his teacher's reaction was to recommend him to a 'special' school instead of giving him more attention. This makes him anxious and stressed to the limit that hair starts falling out even though he's in like third grade so he resorts to cheating and does that all through school until the final year.

- He realises in the final year of highschool that this isn't what he wants to do for the rest of his life so he starts wearing more formal and professional looking clothing (instead of the hipster baggy clothing), shaves his head every day and starts studying and memorizing facts to help him pass. Over a couple of months his reading level improved significantly so he starts practicing for a test called ASVAB that gets him into the Air force which he passes on his third attempt.

- In the Airforce Goggins' struggles with swimming and when his doctor finds out that he has the sickle cell trait Goggins' asks to be reassigned to a role without physical activity (he finds out later that this was a mistake and that he should've continued). Though with a less physically active role his weight baloons up to 300 pounds and then he leaves the air force to working random jobs like a security guard and stuff.

- At one point when he was working as an insect exterminator for different restaurants he goes to one but finds that its significantly filles with insects that they start falling on him so he just leaves his equipment in the restaurant and quits that job, he then went running and realised that he could barely get half a mile. 

- A little later Goggins' saw a video of Navy Seals when they were training and felt sorry for himself that he wasn't one of them but got intensely motivated and driven to become a Navy Seal himself. He contacted many recruiters but almost all would hang up when they find out that he was previously in the military and that he now weighs 300 pounds. Though he found one recruiter that believed in him and over many months he dropped over a hundred pounds to be able to join the navy seals.

- The rest of the story is pretty well known so I won't get into it but the summary is that he became a Seal, joined ranger school, tried out for delta force, became an ultra runner and a triathlete, broke the world record for pull ups in one day and at the end he becomes a wildfire firefighter.
