## Summary
- The book goes over the lives of mainly Wilbur and Orville Wright along with their father the Bishop and their sister Katharine.

- Wilbur 'Will' and Orville 'Orv' get their start in the 'Safety Bicycle' business or just bicycle in modern terms where they built a factory in their hometown of Dayton and they designed and built the bicycles themselves even though neither had graduated from university.

- Over a couple of years they grew the business to a sizeable amount then they hired a man called Charlie Taylor to take car of the bicycle business during the summer while they pursued their passion of making things fly.

- They both enjoyed aircraft and things that fly from a young age and were keen observers of birds and read books by famous authors like Octave Chanute or reading the works of Otto Lilienthal. But they found that the data and theorems they provided was merely theoretical and even at the time the other people in aircraft tended to spend years and a considerable amount of money building a plane before testing it until at the end they realise that their design was flawed.

- The Wright brothers broke this cycle as they had a tiny amount of money to work with so their first prototype costed only a few hundred dollars and was made with easy to source materials. The then looked for suitable places they could test and iterate quickly on their plane with a place that had decent wind speed but the ground was sand so they could land softly. They settled on Kitty Hawk, a beachtown in North Carolina with a tiny population.

- They brought their materials there by train and even struggled to find someone who could navigate them towards Kitty Hawk as it wasn't well known at all. They eventually reached it and settled with a family called the tates until they could build their shed and start working.

- They eventually finished building their prototype and chose to first fly it as a kite then to fly with one of them inside it, though they agreed that they would never fly together as to avoid both of their deaths together which would lead this influential project to end.

- The brothers did this every summer and at the beginning the townspeople were making fun of them but year over year their prototypes improved and could go higher and for longer flights until at one point newspapers and important people started to come to watch the flights.

- They started getting the interest of the French and German governments to Wilbur went on a ship to France and then Germany to showcase their plane in demo flights while Orville kept improving it at home.

- By around 1907 they had been devoting most of their time to airplanes with foreign governments giving them contracts worth tens of thousands of dollars and the brothers spending most of their time outside the U.S allied with a company called Charles R. Flint & Co with a man called Berg who helped them spread their business interests outside the U.S.

- The book continues on about how Wilbur dies from stress from the company that the brothers started to license their invention, Katharine's experience in Europe and how Orville felt about planes being used to kill people in both the World Wars.
