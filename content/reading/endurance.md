## Summary
- The book follows Ernest Shackleton and his and the other 27 crew members' journey trying to cross the south pole by land. Their journey is deemed unfruitful when their ship, Endurance, becomes stuck on a floe in the Weddell sea on their way to Antarctica. 

- The book goes over the crews' struggle through a year and a half trying to get from the Weddell sea on three tiny boats to Elephant Island and eventually to South Georgia Island to get back to society. The expedition itself happened around 1914-17 at the first world war.
