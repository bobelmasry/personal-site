## Summary
- The book goes over Kelly's childhood and how he did wind tunnel testing in college then became interested in planes, then on joining lockheed and designing planes like the sr-71 blackbird there. 

## Notes
- Even though this book is highly praised it felt like the author was rambling about different projects he worked on and there didn't seem to be any structure to it. It also felt like there was too many personal details throughout the book like the woman he was marrying, how he went with them to the doctors and his horses on the ranch. There wasn't much detail given to skunkworks which is the reason most people are probably reading this book.
