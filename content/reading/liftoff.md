## Summary
- The book goes mainly over the first 4 SpaceX launches of the Falcon 1 rocket until it reached orbit. There's a lot of overlap between this book and Ashley Vance's biography on Elon Musk even though there's like a 6 year difference in publication dates. Although this book goes into a little more detail like how they lived on Omelek and Kwaj along with why the most of the key team members in SpaceX left around 2015. 

- The book also goes through interviews with other employees that weren't mentioned in any other biographies or books on SpaceX which is nice.


## Notes

- I didn't really like this book to be honest, Ashlee Vance's style seemed to be missing and the books was too short in my opinion. It also felt weird that it jumped from the first Falcon 9 launch in 2010 to their attempts at landing the booster in 2016 without much in between.

- I hope its sequel "reentry" will be better than this one as its considerably longer.
